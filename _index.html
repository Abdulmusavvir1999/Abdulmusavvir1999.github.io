School Management Projects

This project is aimed at developing a comprehensive School Management System designed to streamline critical school operations, with a primary focus on Curriculum Management. The system is structured to support a broad range of features that enhance administrative efficiency and academic planning. These include :

  • Level-Based Curriculum Structure: Design and implement a tiered structure for easy subject assignments and teacher management.
  • Teacher and Subject Assignment: Assign teachers to specific subjects to maintain an organized academic environment.
  • Staff and Student Management: Create profiles for staff and students, allowing better management of their information, attendance, and activities.
  • Attendance Tracking: Record and monitor attendance for both staff and students.
  • Leave Management: Handle leave requests, including monthly leave limits, paid/unpaid leave, and holidays.
  • Payroll and Salary Deduction: Automate payroll calculations with deductions for taxes, absences, and other reasons.
  • Certificate, ID Card, and Report Card Generation: Generate certificates, ID cards, and report cards with customizable templates and a preview feature.
  • Roles and Permissions: Implement role-based access control to manage permissions for different types of users (admins, teachers, staff, students).

Front-End: HTML, CSS, Tailwind, TS

Back-End: Node.js, Express.js, MySQL, ERP Diagram

Framework: Angular
